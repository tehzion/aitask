# AiTask Future Improvement Roadmap

This roadmap separates immediate quality priorities from future opportunities. None of the future items below should be treated as already delivered.

## Immediate priorities

| Priority | Focus | Intended outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Close full browser regression and visual-baseline follow-ups. | A more complete and repeatable release gate. |
| 2 | Measure real-device and large-workspace performance. | Evidence-based targets for mobile responsiveness and authenticated startup. |
| 3 | Agree management KPIs. | A shared way to measure workload, overdue work, review turnaround, and service follow-through. |
| 4 | Complete the disposable database rollout rehearsal. | Greater confidence in migration rehearsal and recovery procedures. |
| 5 | Review administrative security settings and monitoring. | Stronger ongoing protection and faster detection of unusual behaviour. |

## Future opportunities

### Enhanced client portal

Extend the existing client-facing experience with clearer project progress, approval status, feedback history, and deliverable visibility. The design must preserve the separation between client-visible information and internal notes, drafts, files, and staff-only data.

### Selected integrations

Evaluate calendar, communication, document, and business-system integrations where they remove duplicate work. Each integration should have an agreed owner, data scope, failure-handling approach, and permission model before implementation.

### Additional automation

Consider controlled automation for recurring task creation, reminders, escalation, renewal follow-up, and management summaries. Automation should be idempotent, auditable, and easy for authorised users to review or correct.

### Operational reporting

Develop agreed reporting definitions for project health, milestone progress, service delivery, department workload, client approvals, and follow-up performance. Reports should be based on definitions accepted by management rather than inferred from technical fields.

### Continued maintainability work

Continue modularizing the largest application areas after explicit workflow contracts and regression coverage are in place. This will help Infinitas deliver future enhancements with lower change risk.

## Planning principle

Infinitas recommends prioritizing the next improvement by business constraint: observe the operational issue, agree the desired outcome, implement the smallest safe change, verify it, and review the result with the client.
