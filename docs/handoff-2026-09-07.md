# Handoff — AiTask v2.1.5 HOD task ownership

Captured: 2026-09-07. This handoff records the implementation and local assurance state; production promotion remains gated.

## Scope

- Added the protected Staff-based HOD role (`system-hod`) with `manageCreatedTasks`.
- HODs can view and manage tasks they create, including after reassignment, and can work on tasks assigned to them under ordinary Staff restrictions.
- HODs cannot view or change unrelated tasks, move work into a department they do not belong to, or change a task creator.
- Standard Admins retain all-task visibility but can edit only created or assigned tasks. Boss Koo retains unrestricted task control.
- Existing workspaces receive the protected role without automatic member promotion or task/data transformation.
